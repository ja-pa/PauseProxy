PAUSEPROXY / EXPERIMENT

Try a short pause before spending more time on the web.

Do you want to continue?

Before you decide, solve a short math problem.

Write at least 3 words and 12 letters in total, without just repeating words or letters. For example, “I want to reply to a message.” Your answer is not saved.

Take a moment to consider whether you want to continue. Start the pause and reflect while it runs. Both your answer and the pause must be complete.

10 s

Time only counts while this page is visible. Switching away pauses the countdown.

In PauseProxy, you would now continue using the website.

Standalone prototype. The button only shows a confirmation. No website is unblocked.
